T﻿his post will serve as an outline for a talk about redesigning our giving form at the University of Cincinnati Foundation.

Issues with old giving form:

* S﻿low loading of funds.
* Cognitive overload due to all fields showing on the page.
* A﻿dding fund(s) not intuitive.
* N﻿o real-time error checking.
* N﻿o default amounts to select.
* R﻿eview page felt disjointed from giving form. Occurrences of double giving to same fund.

U﻿pgrades to redesigned giving form:

* M﻿ulti-step donation experience.
* D﻿efault donation options for One-time and Recurring gift types.
* F﻿uzzy fund search. Allows for typos for hard to spell funds.
* R﻿eal-time error checking.
* C﻿ommon questions section answers most frequent inquieries received by Gift Processing team.
* A﻿ddress autocomplete saves user time by reducing the amount of typing needed. It prevents common typos in address information allowing for more accurate information.
* P﻿hone number validation checks to ensure phone number is entered in the correct format based on country/area code selected.
* R﻿emoved Review page to check out.
* N﻿avigation buttons ("back" and "continue") allow user to updates to form.
* P﻿rogress bar at top increases donor motivation and conversation rates.



**C﻿ontent Structure:**

* **Landscape:** what exists. In my presentation, the landscape was “A lot of web pages load slowly.”
* **Analysis:** what you, the presenter, want to highlight for the audience about that landscape. “This poor performance creates a poor user experience.”
* **Problem:** the core issue based on your analysis. “Studies have shown a correlation between a slow site and decreased engagement, which isn’t what website owners want.”
* **Options:** what we could do. “We could ignore it! Or we could speed up the site in these ways.”
* **Solution:** the best option and how it works. This was the bulk of my talk, walking through all the ways to improve front-end performance.
* **Reasons:** why the audience should believe you. “Completing this work sped up the site by 35% and increased conversions by 7%.”
* **Bigger** **idea:** why this concept matters even if it seems irrelevant to an audience member’s work. “Even if you think your site is fast, what’s the experience for your users on slower infrastructure, outdated mobile networks, or older devices?”
